## Solo scales

Key: D major
- D major (D E F# G A B C#) — safe default, the song's home
- D major pentatonic (D E F# A B) — simplest default, plain folk feel
- B minor pentatonic (B D E F# A) — darker colour, leans into the Bm at the end of the chorus line
- Harmonica: D harp, 1st position (straight harp), in the original's style
