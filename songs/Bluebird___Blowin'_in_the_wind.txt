[Guidelines]
Solo scales:

The chord set in your file (D, G, A, Bm, Dsus) is straight D major, so
soloing options:

  - D major scale:
    — D E F# G A B C# D
    - The default. Fits every chord in the song, gives a melodic,
      folk-singer feel. Good for following the vocal phrasing.
  - D major pentatonic:
    — D E F# A B
    - Same flavour, fewer notes, almost impossible to play a wrong
      one. Best starting point if you just want to riff over the
    changes.
  - B minor pentatonic:
    — B D E F# A
    - The relative minor, exact same notes as D major pentatonic but
    rooted on B. Gives a more pensive, slightly bluesy ache. Leans
    into the contemplative Dylan tone.
  - D mixolydian:
    - D E F# G A B C
    — only if you drop the C# to a C natural; gives a
      more modal, drone-y folk colour. Works over D and G but clashes a
      touch with the A chord (which wants C#). Use sparingly.

Practical pick: noodle in D major pentatonic for the verse, slide into
B minor pentatonic  when you want the line to sigh, and pull in the C#
from the full D major scale when you land on or pass through the A chord.

If you play harmonica (Dylan's solo instrument here), a G harp played
in 2nd position gives you D mixolydian naturally — that's the classic
sound on the original.
