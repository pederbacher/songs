## Solo scales
Key: A minor (modal — built on the Am–G vamp)
- A natural minor / Aeolian (A B C D E F G) — safe default, dark shanty feel
- A minor pentatonic (A C D E G) — simplest default, can't miss over the riff
- C major pentatonic (C D E G A) — relative major, brighter lift for a contrasting pass
- A Dorian (A B C D E F# G) — modal option; the raised F# over the G is the classic "Drunken Sailor" colour
- Fiddle / tin whistle: stay in A Dorian and lean on the F# against the G for the sea-shanty bounce
