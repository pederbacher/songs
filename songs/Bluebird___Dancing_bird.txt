## Solo scales

Key: A major

- A major scale (A B C# D E F# G#) — safe default, bright and sunny
- A major pentatonic (A B C# E F#) — simplest default, breezy dance-floor leads
- F# minor pentatonic (F# A B C# E) — relative minor, a contrasting colour
- E Mixolydian (E F# G# A B C# D) — modal option, lifts the bright E chords in the chorus
